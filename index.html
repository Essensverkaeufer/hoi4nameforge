What the parser reads

It reads name, texturefile, noOfFrames and legacy_lazy_load from spriteType, frameAnimatedSpriteType, textSpriteType, corneredTileSpriteType, progress-bar and shield blocks, including nested ones, and strips GFX_ from the name. Imported sprites have no preview image.

Frame tester experimental in name fields

Builds every frame variant of a multi-frame sprite so you can paste it in and see which frames work.

The v2 claim was wrong

v2 said only texticons.gfx entries work. A player used TC_heavy_tank_artillery_chassis_fra, the tank-designer blueprint of the French heavy SPG from tank_modules_blueprint_overlay.gfx.

The pattern: all 472 flagged sprites in texticons.gfx and that blueprint have legacy_lazy_load = no, so their textures are always in memory. Focus icons are lazy-loaded, and players report they don't render. "Textable" most likely means "always loaded", so the library filters on that flag by default.

DLC sprites probably only render for players who own that DLC. The game may also limit name length, and control characters count toward it.
